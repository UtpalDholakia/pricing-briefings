Decisions for Utpal

Everything in the research base that is waiting on you, with a recommendation where an agent has made one. The queue is rebuilt from the data files every time the site builds. Rules: AGENTS.md section 14.

How to decide. Click a choice on any item (★ marks the recommendation; click it again to undo), or use Accept all ★ on a section. Your choices are kept in this browser until you send them. When you're done, click Send to Claude at the bottom: it opens a pre-filled GitHub issue, and you click Submit new issue. Claude applies everything in its next daily run, lists it under Recently decided and closes the issue. You can decide a few items at a time.

Your decisions as text. Copy this and paste it into the GitHub issue (or send it to Claude in chat).

Sent. Submit the issue on the GitHub tab that opened; the items stay on this page, marked as sent, until Claude's next run applies them.
