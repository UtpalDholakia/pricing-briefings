Hypotheses

What the daily briefings are hunting for. Each hypothesis is a falsifiable prediction plus the observable test that would settle it. Every morning each agent picks up to two in its lanes, searches for them and reports hits and misses. A hit files evidence; enough evidence graduates a hypothesis into a claim; three evidence-free hunts trigger a review. Rules: AGENTS.md section 13.
