Everything the research base knows about how a firm prices, assembled from the trackers, the AI pricing database, the evidence file and the claims register each time the site builds. A firm gets a dossier at 3 entries; the registry is _data/firms.yml (AGENTS.md section 11). Nothing here is typed by hand: correct the tracker entry, not the dossier.
