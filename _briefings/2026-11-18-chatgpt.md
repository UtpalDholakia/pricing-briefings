## Key takeaways

- Loganair is keeping more lower-priced fares available until departure. Its headline reduction applies to the highest short-notice fares, not the average ticket.
- Disney’s fan club is adding a cheaper way to get paid access while raising the price of the tier that includes a physical kit.
- In a simulated shop, the cheapest product was more often picked as the best deal when discount labels used the seller’s recent low as the basis of comparison.

## What happened

### 1. Loganair keeps lower fares in play closer to departure
**Source:** [Loganair fare announcement](https://www.loganair.co.uk/news/loganair-ceo-commits-to-more-affordable-fares-for-more-people-more-of-the-time/), November 17, 2026.

On services to and from Orkney, Shetland and Stornoway, Loganair says it will make more lower-priced fares available right up until departure. It reports cuts of up to 23% in the highest short-notice fares on the affected routes. Its lowest fares remain available to people who book ahead. The airline cites feedback about the cost of short-notice island travel. It has not reported what passengers paid after the change.

**Tags:** Dynamic pricing, Affordability, Fairness.

### 2. Disney puts a separate price on fan-club access
**Sources:** [D23’s 2026 membership plans](https://support.d23.com/hc/en-us/articles/48286116372499-What-are-the-D23-2026-Membership-Plans), updated March 26, 2026; [D23’s 2027 plan details](https://support.d23.com/hc/en-us/articles/57649003520531-What-are-the-new-2027-Membership-Plans), updated November 17, 2026.

From January 1, D23’s $39.99 annual Access plan includes early access to buy select event tickets but no mailed kit. Essential, which includes a kit, goes from $49.99 to $54.99 a year. The new entry tier gives fans who mainly want access a lower price. Members who want the kit face a $5 increase. One menu does both.

**Tags:** Membership, Tiered Pricing, Price Bundling.

### 3. UPS changes the formula behind its fuel fee
**Sources:** [UPS fuel surcharge notice and billing rules](https://www.ups.com/us/en/support/shipping-support/shipping-costs-rates/fuel-surcharges), table effective November 16, 2026; [EIA diesel series](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/pet_pri_gnd_a_epd2d_pte_dpgal_w.htm), released November 17, 2026.

UPS revised its fuel tables while retaining a weekly diesel index for domestic ground. The national diesel reading fell from $6.127 on November 9 to $5.937 on November 16. Applying both readings to the new table gives 30.00% and 29.50%, respectively. That is a schedule calculation holding the formula fixed. UPS says its posted weekly percentages take precedence. Neither figure is evidence of what a particular shipper paid.

**Tags:** B2B pricing, Price transparency, Partitioned Pricing.

### 4. The same sale price looks different against a different base
**Source:** [European Commission experiment report](https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/1af18b1f-c77e-11f1-a77b-01aa75ed71a1/language-en), released October 13, 2026, Experiment 2, Figure 10.

In a simulated shop, 50.8% of participants picked the cheapest of three products as the best deal when the percent-off label and crossed-out price used a higher comparison price. That rose to 80.6% when both used the seller’s lowest price in the previous 30 days. The test changed both display elements together. It measured choices on a screen, not paid purchases.

**Tags:** Academic research, Price presentation, Price Promotions, Consumer decision making.

### 5. CLEAR puts a three-day offer behind an app download
**Source:** [CLEAR’s holiday promotion announcement](https://ir.clearme.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/202/great-holiday-travel-does-exist-clear-unveils-a-smarter), November 12, 2026.

For December 1–3, CLEAR says new members can buy a first year of CLEAR+ for $149 instead of the advertised regular $219, but only in the app. Holiday guest-pass recipients can use the offer too. The promotion makes the discount contingent on adopting another channel. A burst of signups would not, on its own, show whether the app requirement helped.

**Tags:** Price Promotions, Membership, Subscription Renewals.

## Why it matters

A traveler who has to fly tomorrow cannot act on the cheapest advance fare. For that customer, availability within the booking window matters more than the advertised starting price. D23 changes the comparison in a different way. Members can now put a price on getting in without paying for the collectibles. The European experiment shows why the baseline belongs next to the saving: the discount label can pull attention away from the price the shopper actually pays.

## Ideas to apply

- Track the lowest bookable fare at the same lead time on the same route, including how often the fare is available.
- When introducing a cheaper membership, separate downgrades from new signups. Check which benefits each group actually uses before judging the change by plan mix.
- For an app-only discount, compare an equivalent offer on the web. Measure activated members and first renewal as well as checkout conversion.
