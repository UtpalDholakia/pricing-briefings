## Key takeaways

- A promise about the next price can matter to someone deciding whether to buy today. Best Buy Canada offers a refund remedy, but customers must request it.
- DoorDash distinguishes a common item price from personalized discounts. Shoppers may care about the final difference, not just how it was created.
- A subscription menu affects whether people join at all, not only which term they choose.

## What happened

### 1. Best Buy Canada gives early buyers a conditional safety net
**Source:** [Best Buy Canada’s holiday price guarantees](https://www.bestbuy.ca/en-ca/help/holiday-shopping-at-best-buy/making-sure-you-get-the-best-price), sale beginning November 19, 2026; checked November 20.

Products with a Black Friday Price badge are promised not to fall in price during the retailer’s Black Friday sale, including November 27. If Best Buy lowers the price, buyers can request the difference through its price-match process. This is not an automatic adjustment. The promise addresses the regret of buying before a later reduction. It does not establish that the item is at its lowest historical price or the lowest price elsewhere.

**Tags:** Price Promotions, Communicating prices, Consumer decision making.

### 2. DoorDash defends personalized discounts, not personalized item prices
**Source:** [DoorDash’s pricing principles](https://about.doordash.com/en-us/news/doordash-announces-pricing-principles), November 16, 2026.

DoorDash says two people ordering from the same store at the same time see the same item price. It also describes discounts based on previous orders. That separates the common price from the personalized reduction. Its no-real-time-surge commitment includes a disclosed bad-weather fee exception. These are the company’s statements, not an independent price audit. A useful consumer test would hold final prices constant and vary whether the difference is presented as a discount or a higher price.

**Tags:** Price discrimination, Fairness, Price transparency.

### 3. NVIDIA offers a large discount on a short period
**Source:** [NVIDIA’s GeForce NOW promotion](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/geforce-now-thursday-black-friday-2026/), November 19, 2026.

NVIDIA has brought back six-month Performance and Ultimate memberships with 30% off the first term for new memberships and eligible upgrades in selected regions. Separately, purchases through Amazon on supported Fire TV devices get 90% off the first month in participating regions through December 31. Comparing the percentages alone misses the payment period and eligibility. One subsidizes a six-month commitment. The other makes the first month unusually cheap. The announcement does not report conversion or renewal results.

**Tags:** subscription pricing, Price Promotions, Pricing models.

### 4. PriceSmart takes payment now, but starts the Chile membership year later
**Sources:** [PriceSmart’s expansion announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pricesmart-begins-membership-sales-in-chile-advancing-its-expansion-to-a-14th-market-302944760.html), November 19, 2026; [Banco de Chile’s partnership terms](https://sitiospublicos.bancochile.cl/nuestro-banco/es/prensa/noticias/detalle/pricesmart-y-banco-de-chile-sellan-alianza-comercial-ante-la-llegada-del-primer-club-de-compras-de-la-cadena-al-pais-y-se-inicia-la-venta-de-membrecias), November 10.

PriceSmart began selling Chile memberships on November 10 before opening its first local club in 2027. Banco de Chile says annual membership starts at CLP 55,000. Founding members can use existing international clubs immediately, but their twelve-month term in Chile starts when the first club opens. That avoids using up the local access period while the customer waits. It does not remove the uncertainty about when the store will open.

**Tags:** Membership, Customer loyalty, Pricing models.

### 5. The long-term discount can change who subscribes
**Source:** [Tian and Feinberg, subscription selectivity field experiment](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2020.1265), published October 21, 2020.

An older discovery: a dating-site experiment varied the overall price level separately from the steepness of discounts for longer contracts. The authors modeled joining and plan choice together. Their estimated optimum put prices higher, particularly for the longest plan. That is a model-based conclusion for this firm, not a subsequent rollout result. Its practical lesson is methodological: optimizing the share selecting an annual plan can miss people the menu discouraged from joining.

**Tags:** Academic research, subscription pricing, Pricing models.

## Why it matters

The relevant comparison can be across dates, across buyers or across contract lengths. A price guarantee reduces one source of uncertainty, while a request requirement leaves work for the buyer. A low introductory price changes the first payment, not necessarily the longer-run cost. Keep those promises separate when testing an offer.

## Ideas to apply

- Test the price guarantee against the same offer without it. Measure purchase timing and adjustment requests, not just headline conversion.
- Show the amount due for the full introductory period alongside the subsequent billing terms. Keep channel eligibility visible.
- Evaluate a subscription menu using all eligible visitors. Report joining, plan choice and retained revenue separately.
