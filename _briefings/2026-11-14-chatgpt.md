## Key takeaways

- The easiest TikTok Shop coupon to use covers only about half the monthly fee. Break-even can depend on buying more. See item 1.
- A room discount matters alongside the cancellation terms. Compare dollar totals, not just advertised maximums. See item 3.
- A pick-your-own bundle needs an estimate of which products people will put together, not just their average cost. See item 5.

## What happened

### 1. TikTok Shop charges $9.99 for a basket of coupons

**Sources:** [Hello Partner · Nov 12, 2026](https://hellopartner.com/tiktok-shop-brings-coupons-and-loyalty-in-house-with-new-paid-membership/) · [Modern Retail · Nov 9, 2026](https://www.modernretail.co/operations/exclusive-tiktok-shop-launches-a-paid-premium-tier-for-1m-of-its-top-us-shoppers/)

TikTok Shop is piloting a $9.99 monthly membership for eligible U.S. shoppers after a 30-day trial. One monthly coupon takes $5 off without a minimum. Two others take $10 off at $59 and $30 off at $119. The least restrictive coupon alone covers only about half the fee.

### 2. New York City’s Council draws a line at personal data

**Sources:** [New York City Council · Nov 12, 2026](https://council.nyc.gov/press/2026/11/12/3339/) · [Legislative record](https://legistar.council.nyc.gov/LegislationDetail.aspx?GUID=A6815BBB-AC2B-487F-A27B-46449A644474&ID=8027985&Options=ID%7CText%7C)

The legislative record shows Council passage. At the time of this briefing, it did not list an enactment date.

### 3. What is the option to cancel a hotel room worth?

**Sources:** [The Points Guy · Nov 13, 2026; Sonesta offer terms](https://thepointsguy.com/deals/points-and-miles-travel-deals) · [Sonesta offer terms](https://www.sonesta.com/offers/sonesta-holiday-travel-presale)

Sonesta’s member presale advertises up to 30% off a prepaid, nonrefundable rate and up to 25% off a rate with standard cancellation terms. Both sit under the same promotion. The five-point difference is between advertised maximums. It does not establish the actual gap for any particular room.

**Tags:** Hospitality, Price transparency

### 4. Ritual’s 40% discount ends before the subscription does

**Sources:** [Ritual email via Milled · Nov 11, 2026](https://milled.com/ritual/the-only-40-off-sale-in-the-world-O0fdH-GXQ4ahF6aj) · [Ritual terms](https://ritual.com/pages/2026-black-friday-sale-terms-and-conditions)

Ritual’s Black Friday email offers 40% off the first order of a new subscription. Future orders automatically renew at full retail price until canceled. New customers can also get a water bottle with a qualifying starter set of at least two products. The email dates the sale from November 12 to December 1.

**Tags:** Subscription Renewals, subscription pricing

### 5. The expensive item shoppers reach for in a three-for deal

**Source:** [Production and Operations Management · Nov 2, 2026](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/10591478261507272)

A paper by Ruijiu Mao, Shuai Jia, and Chung-Piaw Teo examines fixed-price bundles that let shoppers pick their items. In a case study using one online retailer’s data, choosing eligible items and prices jointly produced higher estimated profit than a simple cost cutoff. The gain came from the model. The authors did not report a field experiment demonstrating it.

**Tags:** Price Bundling, Academic research

## Why it matters

**1. TikTok Shop charges $9.99 for a basket of coupons** A member may start treating the monthly fee as money to earn back. That can make an otherwise unnecessary purchase feel like a saving. Compare what members buy with what equally active nonmembers buy, especially just above the coupon thresholds.

**2. New York City’s Council draws a line at personal data** A shopper can understand why a room costs more on Saturday. Finding out that someone else was quoted less at the same moment raises a different question: What did the seller know about me? Pricing teams should be able to identify and explain the inputs behind a price difference.

**3. What is the option to cancel a hotel room worth?** The cheaper rate asks the traveler to bear the cost of a change in plans. Show the two dollar totals and the cancellation deadline together. That lets shoppers decide what the flexibility is worth for their trip.

**4. Ritual’s 40% discount ends before the subscription does** The first charge sets a reference point. If the regular charge shows up later as a surprise, an acquisition discount can become a retention problem. Put both amounts next to the subscribe button and compare second-order retention, not just sign-ups.

**5. The expensive item shoppers reach for in a three-for deal** Shoppers may build their bundles around the products they value most. Some are expensive to supply. Before setting a bundle price, check what customers are likely to pick together and what that basket costs.

## Ideas to apply

- Look for spending just above coupon thresholds among paid members. Compare with equally active nonmembers to avoid attributing all differences to the program.
- Show the prepaid and flexible room totals next to the cancellation deadline. For subscription offers, show the first charge and the renewal charge together.
- Use the expected mix of items people choose in a bundle to test profit sensitivity before committing to a single bundle price.
