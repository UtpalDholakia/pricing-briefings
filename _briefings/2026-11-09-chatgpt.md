## Key takeaways

- A pair price can change the ranking of tiers without making the same deal available to a solo buyer. See item 1.
- More promotional sign-ups do not establish that the promotional price will work permanently. See item 2.
- Watch for items and trips shoppers stop buying, even when they stay with the same brand. See item 3.

## What happened

### 1. An ice cream museum makes Premium cheaper than General, if you bring someone

Singapore’s Museum of Ice Cream offered two Premium admissions for the price of one from November 9 to 11, with an Instagram code redeemed online. At advertised starting prices, that was S$53 for two, or S$26.50 each, against S$43 each for General Admission. A solo visitor could not get the same saving. (Sources 1, 2.)

**Tags:** Tiered Pricing, Price Promotions, Price Bundling

### 2. Planet Fitness got more sign-ups at $10 and is keeping the $15 price

Management said a brief $10 Classic Card promotion increased sign-ups nationally, but did not deliver enough revenue per club to make the price permanent. Results varied by market. The chain plans to keep Classic at $15 and raise Black Card prices in selected regions in the second quarter of 2027. (Source 3.)

**Tags:** Membership, subscription pricing

### 3. Grocery inflation can shrink the basket without changing the brand

A panel of more than 55,000 German households found that buying less and making fewer shopping trips mattered more during inflation than switching to cheaper brands or stores. Lower-income households cut quantities more. The data cover 2005 to 2019 and may not describe responses to a sharper inflation shock. (Sources 4, 5.)

**Tags:** Affordability, Academic research

### 4. Doing the tip math for customers was associated with higher tips

A Northern European counter-service restaurant added cash amounts beside suggested tip percentages, then reverted to percentages alone. Across 5,169 card transactions, the average tip rose from 1.16% of the bill to 2.47%, then fell to 1.99%. This was a change over time at one restaurant, with no random assignment. (Source 6.)

**Tags:** Price presentation, Restaurant pricing

### 5. A cigarette discount sign can matter before anyone reaches the register

Researchers assigned 289 people aged 18 to 20 to shop in a life-size model convenience store. One group saw cigarette discount information on the entrance door. Those who did not see it were less likely to report openness to future smoking afterward. The study did not measure later smoking or purchases. (Source 7.)

**Tags:** Price Communications, Consumer welfare

## Why it matters

**1. An ice cream museum makes Premium cheaper than General, if you bring someone** The offer changes which tier looks cheapest by making the pair the unit of purchase. Measure how many second guests the offer brings in who would otherwise have stayed home.

**2. Planet Fitness got more sign-ups at $10 and is keeping the $15 price** A promotion tests whether a lower price gets people to act now. It is a weaker test of what those members will pay or how long they will stay. Compare cohorts after the offer ends.

**3. Grocery inflation can shrink the basket without changing the brand** A tight budget can lead shoppers to skip an item or postpone a trip. Stable brand share can therefore coexist with demand loss. Track units and shopping frequency alongside switching.

**4. Doing the tip math for customers was associated with higher tips** Showing the amount may make a voluntary payment easier to evaluate. In a tip-screen test, hold the percentages fixed and measure clarity as well as tips.

**5. A cigarette discount sign can matter before anyone reaches the register** A discount sign can put a product into consideration before a shopper reaches the shelf. Exposure is a separate outcome from redemption. Redemption counts miss people who see a promotion without using it.

## Ideas to apply

- For a pair offer, report the total paid by the pair and the per-person equivalent. Track how many parties buy and how many people attend.
- Follow discounted members beyond their first renewal. Compare retention and revenue per location before resetting the list price.
- On a tip screen, hold suggested percentages fixed if you want to test whether showing the actual amounts changes the decision.
