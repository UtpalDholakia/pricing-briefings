## Key takeaways

- A countdown invites shoppers to infer what they will lose by waiting. See item 1.
- The first price a renter sees can shape which options get compared. See item 2.
- Two packages can make the same bonus easier to use, share, or give away. The study measured draw entry, not sales. See item 5.

## What happened

### 1. Australia is checking whether Black Friday countdowns really end

**Source:** [Australian Competition and Consumer Commission · Nov 10, 2026](https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/accc-warns-against-misleading-%E2%80%98storewide%E2%80%99-or-%E2%80%98limited-time%E2%80%99-black-friday-sales-claims)

Australia’s latest Black Friday advertising sweep will examine emails and social media as well as websites. In a case announced in September, the regulator said Vuly’s timer reached zero on an offer of up to 30% off, then reset as a new offer of up to 45% off began. Vuly paid penalties following infringement notices.

**Tags:** Price Promotions, Regulation

### 2. A rental price can mislead before a renter even applies

**Source:** [Federal Trade Commission · Nov 6, 2026](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/11/progress-residential-owner-pretium-partners-pay-4975-million-settle-ftc-states-charges-over?type=content)

The FTC and four states announced a proposed $49.75 million settlement with Progress Residential and its owner. The complaint alleges that advertised rents omitted mandatory recurring charges, including smart-home or utility-management fees. The proposed order would require the total monthly rent to appear more prominently than any other pricing information.

**Tags:** Partitioned Pricing, Price transparency

### 3. Royal Caribbean makes shoppers explore the ship to unlock a discount

**Sources:** [Royal Caribbean terms · offer opened Nov 9, 2026](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/terms-and-conditions/promotions.Free) · [Quack Quest FAQ](https://www.royalcaribbean.com/faq/questions/what-is-quack-quest)

Royal Caribbean’s Quack Quest offer gives eligible players $50 off inside or ocean-view cabins and $100 off balconies or suites on select Legend of the Seas sailings. Shoppers complete a game that takes them around a virtual version of the ship, then apply a code at checkout.

**Tags:** Incentives, Hospitality

### 4. A Seattle restaurant group ends earning before it ends redemption

**Source:** [One House Hospitality · program ended Nov 9, 2026](https://onehousehospitality.com/esr-rewards-club-terms-and-conditions)

One House Hospitality stopped issuing points after August 9 but let members redeem earned rewards through November 8. Remaining balances expired without cash or credit. Gift cards were unaffected. The group said it would send reminders but did not report how many points were used or forfeited.

**Tags:** Loyalty Program, Rewards Program

### 5. Two bottles can make the same bonus more appealing than one big bottle

**Source:** [Huang et al., Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science · May 9, 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11747-026-01160-6)

In a shampoo experiment, older adults were less likely than younger adults to enter a draw for a chance to buy an oversized bottle. The age gap was not statistically significant with two separate bottles at the same price and total volume. The outcome was draw entry, not a completed purchase.

**Tags:** Academic research, Satiation, Bundles

## Why it matters

**1. Australia is checking whether Black Friday countdowns really end** A clock makes waiting feel like a risk. Check what customers would reasonably expect to lose when it reaches zero, then verify what changes across channels.

**2. A rental price can mislead before a renter even applies** A low headline rent can earn a place on a shortlist that the all-in price would not. Disclosure at signing cannot restore comparisons already skipped. Audit the first price shoppers see, including on third-party listings.

**3. Royal Caribbean makes shoppers explore the ship to unlock a discount** The work required to earn the deal also introduces shoppers to what they would buy. Compare the game with a plain code of equal value to separate the appeal of the virtual tour from the savings.

**4. A Seattle restaurant group ends earning before it ends redemption** Diners may feel they earned part of a future meal. Separate deadlines give members time to use that value without adding to their balances. Look at forfeiture and return visits among members with large balances.

**5. Two bottles can make the same bonus more appealing than one big bottle** Extra quantity has little value if shoppers doubt they will use it. A second bottle is easier to share or give away, one possible explanation. Before making a bonus bigger, test whether making it separable is enough.

## Ideas to apply

- Take a screenshot of a timed offer just before and just after expiry across email, social, and checkout. Check what actually changes.
- Compare an offer unlocked by a virtual tour with a plain code for the same dollar amount. Separate learning about the product from the appeal of the savings.
- If winding down a rewards program, separate the date earning ends from the date balances expire. Track who redeems and who loses a balance.
