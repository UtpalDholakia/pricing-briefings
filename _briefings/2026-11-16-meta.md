## 1. [Microsoft stacks three price changes into one quarter: Power Apps up, Azure up, Copilot metered](https://luby.co/blog/microsofts-2027-price-changes-a-budget-checklist-for-it-leaders/)

**Source:** Luby, US, Nov 12, 2026: [luby.co](https://luby.co/blog/microsofts-2027-price-changes-a-budget-checklist-for-it-leaders/)

Between September 9 and November 11, Microsoft told customers and partners about a Power Apps Premium rise, higher Azure rates in seven regions, a new default for Copilot billing and local-currency adjustments, all landing between January 1 and the end of March 2027. Power Apps Premium rises from $20 to $22 per user per month on January 1, and from $12 to $14 on the 2,000-seat plan, the first list-price rise in more than five years; Azure virtual machines and storage rise 8% to 17% from February 1, with Dublin at 17% and Singapore at 8%. Indian-rupee cloud prices rise 15.7% on January 1. All figures are published list prices; Microsoft has not disclosed what buyers actually pay after negotiation, so net prices are not stated here.

## 2. [Copilot becomes a meter by default for small-business buyers in early 2027](https://www.2tolead.com/insights/microsoft-365-copilot-licensing-changes-december-2026)

**Source:** 2toLead, US, updated Nov 14, 2026: [2tolead.com](https://www.2tolead.com/insights/microsoft-365-copilot-licensing-changes-december-2026)

From December 1, 2026, Microsoft 365 Copilot add-on offers reach end of sale through resellers, and new Copilot offers can be bought without a Microsoft 365 base subscription, at the same price. Between January and March 2027, new Microsoft 365 Copilot Business licences bought through a partner arrive with usage-based billing switched on, with a default limit of 4,000 Copilot Credits per user per month; Microsoft moved this date twice, from November 2 to December 1 and then to early 2027. At $0.01 a credit, the default cap is worth up to $40 per user per month on top of the $30 seat licence, and a new purchase also switches the meter on for licences already in the tenant. This is a mechanism change, not a list-price rise: the seat price holds and the variable part depends on use.

## 3. [Google's Gemini API prices double on December 1, and again on January 1](https://magai.co/gemini-pricing/)

**Source:** Magai, US, checked against Google's pricing pages Nov 10, 2026: [magai.co](https://magai.co/gemini-pricing/)

Google's flagship Gemini 4 Argon costs $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens through November 30, then $4 and $20 from December 1, 2026. The everyday Gemini 3.8 and 3.6 Flash models cost $0.75 and $3.75 through December 31, then $1.50 and $7.50 from January 1, 2027, with cache storage doubling at the same time. Both are introductory rates on Google's own pricing page, so the doubling is the scheduled end of a discount rather than a surprise rise — but any budget built on this autumn's bills doubles for the same work, and the lower Argon rate is delivered as 50% credits back on net spend, not as a lower sticker price.

## 4. [Vietnam's regulator made Grab cut its fees — and pay drivers back](https://stockbiz.vn/en/news/national-competition-commission-has-preliminary-findings-on-grabs-pricing-and-fee-policies/42662241)

**Source:** Stockbiz (VNA), Vietnam, Nov 5, 2026: [stockbiz.vn](https://stockbiz.vn/en/news/national-competition-commission-has-preliminary-findings-on-grabs-pricing-and-fee-policies/42662241)

After drivers told Vietnam's National Competition Commission that fares were falling while fees and deductions stayed put, Grab agreed to six groups of measures from November 10, 2026. The platform fee on short GrabBike rides falls from VND 3,000 to VND 1,000 for trips up to 2 km, and on short GrabCar rides from VND 5,000 to VND 3,000; the app usage fee charged to drivers is capped at 25%, down from as high as 28.76%, and Grab's total share of a fare is capped at 32%. Grab also found about 0.13% of trips since 2024 had deductions above its own settings and is reimbursing about VND 3.9 billion to drivers from November 5, blaming supply-demand swings and algorithm errors.

## 5. [Airtel brought back its Rs 299 plan — with a third less data](https://www.7globe.in/bharti-airtels-rs-299-tariff-hike-u-turn-why-sunil-mittal-led-telco-reversed-the-move-and-why-brokerages-still-see-up-to-15-upside-markets/)

**Source:** 7Globe (ET Now), India, Nov 9, 2026: [7globe.in](https://www.7globe.in/bharti-airtels-rs-299-tariff-hike-u-turn-why-sunil-mittal-led-telco-reversed-the-move-and-why-brokerages-still-see-up-to-15-upside-markets/)

Bharti Airtel reintroduced its Rs 299 prepaid plan in November after scrapping it in August to push customers toward the Rs 349 plan — but the daily data allowance fell from 1.5 GB to 1 GB, so the same price now buys a third less data. The reversal follows a sharp slowdown: net subscriber additions fell from 3.3 million to 0.7 million in a quarter and churn rose from 2.6% to 2.9%, while rival Jio kept its Rs 299 entry plan and added customers. Airtel also raised all postpaid plans by Rs 50 a month from October 8, with the minimum rising from Rs 449 to Rs 499. Brokers still expect an industry-wide tariff rise of 10–15% in early 2027, but no operator has announced one.

## 6. [11.11 is no longer a day — and the vouchers got thinner as the window widened](https://www.misslobang.com/article/best-11-11-sale-deals-singapore)

**Source:** MissLobang, Singapore, updated Nov 16, 2026 (prices read from brands' own pages Nov 4): [misslobang.com](https://www.misslobang.com/article/best-11-11-sale-deals-singapore)

Singapore's 11.11 sales now run well past November 11: Shopee's campaign runs October 30 to November 17 and Lazada's main sale November 10 to 13, with other retailers ending anywhere from November 4 to 15. Shopee's headline is $111 off a $350 spend, but the claimable vouchers were 20% off on October 30 and 16% off, capped at $65 or $250, by November 4, and were already 96–97% claimed between drops. The pricing story is the trade: a longer sale window with a shallower discount, and a voucher pool that runs out, so the best price depends on when you claim rather than what you buy.

## 7. [An eco-friendly seller's Black Friday hot take: no sale, because 20% off is already almost nothing](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dfn3CSPNcGZ/)

**Source:** Instagram, Nov 12, 2026: [instagram.com](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dfn3CSPNcGZ/)

A small eco-friendly cleaning-products seller on Instagram, in her fifth year of business, posted that she will not run a Black Friday sale this year, after years of offering about 15% off and still drawing complaints. She said her business pays staff above the living wage with full healthcare benefits and runs on thin margins, so she cannot and will never offer more than about 20% off — and even at 20% she makes almost nothing. Her argument is the mirror image of the big-retailer discount: if a chain can offer 80% off, it proves how much margin it carries the rest of the year. The reel drew about 28,000 likes.

## 9. [The UK regulator's Microsoft case closed its consultation on November 13 — the price question is next](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/united-kingdom/antitrust-competition-law/the-cma-turns-to-enterprise-software-microsofts-proposed-strategic-market-status)

**Source:** Legal 500, UK, Oct 26, 2026, on the CMA's Oct 16 proposed decision: [legal500.com](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/united-kingdom/antitrust-competition-law/the-cma-turns-to-enterprise-software-microsofts-proposed-strategic-market-status)

On October 16 the UK Competition and Markets Authority proposed designating Microsoft as having strategic market status in its business software ecosystem — Word, Excel, Outlook, Teams, Windows, Windows Server, SQL Server and its security software — provisionally finding substantial and entrenched market power, including a 70–80% UK revenue share in productivity software. The consultation window closed at 5pm on November 13, and a final decision is due by February 13, 2027. The pricing hook is in the roadmap published with the decision: it floats a fair, reasonable and non-discriminatory pricing obligation for Microsoft software whatever cloud hosts it, plus changes to licence transfer rules and possibly unbundling Copilot from existing suites. Designation implies no finding of wrongdoing, and nothing changes for buyers until a conduct requirement is imposed.

## 10. [Small businesses plan price rises at the fastest rate since July 2022](https://www.nfib.com/news/press-release/new-nfib-survey-main-street-optimism-remains-steady-uncertainty-declines-in-october/)

**Source:** NFIB, US, Nov 10, 2026: [nfib.com](https://www.nfib.com/news/press-release/new-nfib-survey-main-street-optimism-remains-steady-uncertainty-declines-in-october/)

The NFIB's October survey of small businesses found a seasonally adjusted net 37% of owners plan to raise prices over the next three months, up 6 points from September and the highest since July 2022, while a net 33% already raised average selling prices, up 2 points and well above the historical average of a net 14%. The headline optimism index barely moved, at 97.8, so the pricing signal sits underneath flat confidence. These are net balances — the share raising prices minus the share cutting them — not a forecast that 37% of firms will raise prices, and the survey's own members cite labour and fuel costs as the pressure behind the move.
