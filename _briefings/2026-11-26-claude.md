## 1. OpenAI calls a 40% faster burn of ChatGPT limits a billing fix, and pays back with one reset

**Source:** Notebookcheck (Nov 26, 2026): [notebookcheck.net](https://www.notebookcheck.net/ChatGPT-users-hit-limits-up-to-40-sooner-after-OpenAI-billing-fix.1460643.0.html); Neowin: [neowin.net](https://www.neowin.net/news/paying-chatgpt-users-will-hit-gpt-62-limits-faster-after-openai-fixes-a-launch-mistake/)

An OpenAI lead for Codex and ChatGPT posted that a launch billing mistake had under-counted GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol usage for most subscribers. After the correction, Go, Plus, Pro and Business users reach their limits about 40% sooner on Astra and 25% sooner on Sol than a week earlier. Prices are unchanged. Nobody is charged retroactively, and everyone gets one banked limit reset. OpenAI's framing does two jobs. Calling the cut an error correction tells users that last week's generous allowance was never the real reference point. Comparing the new limits with GPT-6 and GPT-6.1, not with last week, swaps in an older and lower anchor. Users, though, had a week to adapt to the higher level, so they may code the change as a loss. The banked reset is a concession paired with the explanation, the combination the `apology-needs-restraint` claim says matters. It is also the second time this month that the conversion between work done and allowance used has moved with no price change, two weeks before ChatGPT and Codex begin sharing one token-counted allowance.

## 2. Lidl's Thanksgiving basket is "never beaten on price", but the baskets it is compared with are not the same

**Source:** Money Digest (Nov 2026): [moneydigest.com](https://www.moneydigest.com/2298223/lidl-thanksgiving-dinner-basket-price-match-walmart-aldi-2026/); Grocery Dive: [grocerydive.com](https://www.grocerydive.com/news/thanksgiving-meals-grocery-walmart-target-lidl-aldi-2026/874455/)

Lidl US sold a Thanksgiving basket that feeds ten to Lidl Plus members only (Nov 11-25), one per account. It carried a "Never Beaten on Price" guarantee that the whole basket would cost no more than comparable bundles at Walmart ($35) or Aldi (about $39). The guarantee covers the basket total, not individual items, and the baskets differ. Aldi's has a 16-pound turkey to Lidl's 14 pounds, and the item lists do not match. Even the reported price varies ($34.40 or $35.86). A price-match promise works as a signal: shoppers infer low prices from the seller's willingness to be checked. A guarantee across non-matching bundles keeps the signal while making the check almost impossible. Gating the price behind the loyalty app makes it a price for app users, and it collects their data. Filed as an open question on whether an unverifiable basket guarantee builds price image as well as an item-level match.

## 3. Ryanair's free seat now arrives after check-in, a few hours before take-off

**Source:** Travelers Today (Nov 14, 2026): [travelerstoday.com](https://www.travelerstoday.com/articles/61264/20261114/ryanair-free-check-extends-72-hours-your-seat-arrives-much-later.htm); Alba24: [alba24.ro](https://alba24.ro/ryanair-schimba-regulile-pentru-pasagerii-care-nu-platesc-alegerea-locului-in-avion-cand-vor-afla-ce-scaun-au-primit-1166910.html)

Ryanair widened free online check-in for passengers who do not buy a seat from 24 to 72 hours before departure. It looks like a gift, but the random seat is now allocated after check-in, not at check-in. Passengers report seeing their seat number about three hours before the flight, which Ryanair has not confirmed for all flights. Airport check-in and bag drop also close 60 minutes before departure from Nov 10 (was 40), and paid-seat buyers can still check in 60 days out. No price moved. The free option was degraded, a version of the damaged-goods strategy: uncertainty about whether a group sits together becomes the reason to pay. The longer window gives the change a generous frame while the free product gets worse.

## 4. New York's surveillance-pricing ban is now a fight over whether a personal discount counts as a personal price

**Source:** Spectrum News (Nov 23, 2026): [spectrumlocalnews.com](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/nys/central-ny/ny-state-of-politics/2026/11/23/one-fair-price-act); Business Council of NYS memo: [bcnys.org](https://www.bcnys.org/sites/default/files/2026-05/FINAL_NO%20DISCOUNTS%20ONE%20PAGER.pdf); CBS6 Albany: [cbs6albany.com](https://cbs6albany.com/news/power-and-politics/business-council-of-nys-discusses-surveillance-pricing-pushing-for-governor-hochul-veto)

New York's One Fair Price Act passed both chambers in June. It is still unsigned, and Governor Hochul has until Dec 31 to sign it, veto it or let it become law. Some trackers wrongly list it as signed. The Business Council and a trade coalition are pressing for a veto or amendments. They argue that the bill would sweep in personalised promotions and loyalty offers, and want discounts removed from its scope entirely. The sponsor and Attorney General James say bona fide discounts are protected. The dispute turns on framing. The same data-driven price difference reads as a gain when called a member discount and as a loss when called a surcharge on everyone else. The law's wording has to say which frame defines a "personalised price". Updated in place on the existing `ny-algorithmic-pricing-label-2026` laws entry.

## 5. A discount explained as ad money returned to shoppers is judged more legitimate, and that lifts purchase intention

**Source:** Kim et al., Journal of Consumer Behaviour (online Oct 16, 2026): [onlinelibrary.wiley.com](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/cb.70267)

Ad-funded discounts are spreading: watch an ad, see a sponsored placement, pay less. Across two experiments, explaining such a discount as a fair exchange, with advertising revenue passed back to the shopper, raised its perceived legitimacy compared with presenting it as an ordinary promotion. Legitimacy in turn raised purchase intention. Inferred manipulative intent mattered less consistently and depended on context. The finding fits motive attribution: a discount that does not say why it exists invites a cynical reading, and one that names its funding source heads that off. The evidence is stated intentions only. Sample sizes and effect sizes were not readable in the abstract.
