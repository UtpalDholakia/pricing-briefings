## Key takeaways

- A dated ticket changes the commitment even when the highest and lowest prices stay put. See item 1.
- Making points easier to spend is a different change from helping customers earn them faster. See item 2.
- A wholesale discount can send a signal about what buyers will be able to resell. The experiment did not establish the reason for the response. See item 5.

## What happened

### 1. Hong Kong Disneyland changes what a ticket commits you to

Guests now select a visit date when buying a ticket. Direct purchases also reserve admission for that day. Eligible unused tickets can be moved online, subject to availability and any applicable price difference. The highest and lowest ticket prices remain unchanged. (Sources 1, 2.)

**Tags:** Contracts, Customer Experience

### 2. KFC makes the same points balance useful for more meals

KFC announced double points on eligible digital orders from November 11 to 30. It also expanded its redemption catalog from about a dozen items to 24 across four point tiers. At 500 points, members can now choose a pot pie or three tenders, among other additions. (Source 3.)

**Tags:** Loyalty Program, Rewards Program

### 3. Great Clips lets one customer pass a free haircut to another

On Veterans Day, veterans and active-duty service members could get a free haircut at Great Clips or take a card for a later visit. Other customers could receive a free-haircut card to give to an eligible person. The cards were redeemable through December 4. (Sources 4, 5.)

**Tags:** Price Promotions, Social processes

### 4. A familiar brand did not make shoppers give up warranty protection

In a U.S. choice experiment involving laptops and washing machines, moving from no warranty to one year raised the likelihood of choosing the product by about 10 percentage points. There was no evidence that a more familiar brand made respondents more willing to accept weaker protections. These were experimental choices, not store purchases. (Source 6.)

**Tags:** Academic research, Branding, Consumer welfare

### 5. A wholesale discount made Finnish drugstores less likely to order

A Finnish wholesaler randomly offered drugstore buyers a new mouthwash at its regular price or at 20% off. The discount reduced the estimated odds of ordering by 47%. In separate interviews, buyers questioned whether a discounted launch meant the product would be hard to resell. The field experiment measured one launch and did not establish why buyers passed. (Source 7.)

**Tags:** B2B pricing, launch pricing, Discounting

## Why it matters

**1. Hong Kong Disneyland changes what a ticket commits you to** A date choice adds commitment at checkout. A clear change policy can reduce the perceived risk of choosing too early. Track booking abandonment alongside reservation errors.

**2. KFC makes the same points balance useful for more meals** Earning faster and finding something worth redeeming solve different problems. More choice can make an existing balance feel more valuable without cutting the threshold. With both changes introduced together, it is hard to attribute any increase in orders to either alone.

**3. Great Clips lets one customer pass a free haircut to another** A transferable card lets someone else bring the offer to an eligible customer. That can make the gesture personal, though the handoff is also another point where redemption can fail. Count gifted cards redeemed separately from cards issued.

**4. A familiar brand did not make shoppers give up warranty protection** A warranty can make protection concrete in a way reputation alone may not. When testing a premium price, show warranty terms explicitly instead of assuming the brand communicates the same assurance.

**5. A wholesale discount made Finnish drugstores less likely to order** A lower acquisition cost can come with an unfavorable signal about sell-through. Test whether explaining the reason for a launch allowance changes how inventory buyers interpret it.

## Ideas to apply

- Measure abandonment after the date-selection step separately from complaints about reservation errors.
- Track gifted offer cards issued and redeemed as a distinct cohort. The gap is where a transferable promotion can lose reach.
- For a new product sold through a wholesaler, test a clear explanation for a launch allowance before assuming a bigger discount will improve take-up.
