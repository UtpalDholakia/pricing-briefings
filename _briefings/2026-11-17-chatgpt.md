## Key takeaways

- The amount of a price match can stay the same while the form of repayment changes what it’s worth.
- Landmark plans to make annual paid members choose a new tier rather than renew them automatically into one.
- The Marugame price cut comes with a specific theory about what diners might buy next. The company hasn’t tested it publicly.

## What happened

### 1. Booking.com changes how it can pay a price match

**Sources:** [Current U.S. terms](https://www.booking.com/content/terms.html) · Updated August 25, 2025 · [New U.S. terms](https://q-xx.bstatic.com/static/docs/tos-hww/Customer_Terms_of_Service_US_December_2026.pdf) · Dated December 10, 2026 · [QuietlyChanged](https://quietlychanged.com/changes/2026-11-08-booking-com-price-match-will-pay-wallet-credits-not-a-refund/) · November 8, 2026

Booking.com’s current U.S. terms promise to refund the difference on a qualifying price match. The December terms say the company may adjust the reservation price where possible or give instructions for claiming the difference as wallet credits after the stay. The new clause does not say whether those credits can be cashed out. The documents establish a wording change, not how individual claims have been paid.

**Tags:** Price Promotions, Customer loyalty

### 2. Best Buy discounts the spending balance instead of a product

**Source:** [Best Buy’s Black Friday announcement](https://corporate.bestbuy.com/2026/black-friday-sale-deals/) · November 17, 2026

On November 27, Best Buy plans to sell $100 gift cards for $60 in stores. Free loyalty members qualify. The offer is limited to one per person, with at least 50 cards per store while supplies last. The card can be used toward eligible purchases. The saving is $40 for a member who uses the full balance. The offer does not establish how much of that spending would have happened anyway.

**Tags:** Price Promotions, Incentives

### 3. Landmark turns renewal into a fresh choice

**Source:** [Landmark Theatres loyalty FAQ](https://www.landmarktheatres.com/new-loyalty-faq/) · Undated preview, checked November 17, 2026

Landmark’s preview describes a free tier and two paid tiers. Existing annual Loyalty Lover members keep their benefits until the end of their term, then must select a new tier. Their old membership will not renew automatically under the new program. Existing free members keep their current benefits for six months after the transition. The FAQ does not give a launch date and leaves final billing terms to launch.

**Tags:** Membership, Subscription Renewals, Loyalty Program

### 4. A cheaper bowl of udon leaves room for tempura

**Source:** [Marugame Seimen’s announcement](https://pdf.irpocket.com/C3397/xoA3/DnNg/JEYI/Umpi.pdf) · November 12, 2026

From November 25, Marugame Seimen plans to cut its basic kake udon from ¥440 to ¥390 in Japan. Four other items also get reductions. The chain says it is keeping quantities and ingredients unchanged. Its explanation is specific: a cheaper bowl gives diners more room to choose the tempura they want. That is the company’s rationale. It has yet to report whether customers add more sides.

**Tags:** QSR Pricing, Price Communications

### 5. A coupon can make the seller’s motives part of the decision

**Sources:** [Koo, Lalwani and Sun, Journal of Marketing](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/00222429261458261?mi=ehikzz) · June 3, 2026 · [Research summary](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-shoppers-coupons.html) · November 17, 2026

Across field evidence and experiments, consumers who were more accepting of unequal power were less likely to use coupons. The authors link part of the difference to skepticism about the seller’s motives. A price-match guarantee narrowed the gap in the research. The results do not imply that a guarantee will raise redemption everywhere or that geography is a reliable stand-in for an individual’s beliefs.

**Tags:** Academic research, Price Promotions, Fairness

## Why it matters

A credit is easier to value when the customer already expects to spend it. That is a plausible reason Best Buy can make a gift card feel like an immediate saving while a post-stay credit might make a price guarantee feel weaker. Neither inference is a measured result.

Landmark’s change also affects what the firm can learn. Choosing to pay again is different from letting an existing subscription renew. A decline in paid memberships during a migration would not, by itself, tell you whether customers disliked the new prices or simply did not make a choice.

## Ideas to apply

- Test a refund against an equal credit. Measure how customers value the guarantee before purchase and what they actually redeem afterward.
- Test a lower entry price on one menu item. Track contribution per order and whether more diners add sides.
- In a membership migration, separate paid-tier selection, moves to the free tier, and customers who made no selection.
