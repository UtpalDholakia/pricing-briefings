## Key takeaways

- A Thanksgiving basket can cost less in total and more per guest. The serving assumptions matter.
- Halloween markdowns were shallower as more items sold out. That does not establish that smaller discounts increased profit.
- A payment method can change spending without moving stated willingness to pay by the same amount.

## What happened

### 1. Halloween shoppers face fewer discounts and more sold-out costumes

**Source:** [Lipper Alpha Insight analysis](https://lipperalpha.refinitiv.com/2026/10/halloween-2026-fewer-tricks-fewer-discounts-but-consumers-still-treat-themselves/) · October 30, 2026

Average online discounts on Halloween costumes and accessories fell from 36% to 15% year over year. Meanwhile, 11% of tracked Halloween apparel items were completely sold out, versus 2% last year. Inventory differences could explain part of that pattern. These observations do not establish that shallower discounts increased profitability.

**Tags:** Discounting, High demand

### 2. Thanksgiving value looks different per basket and per guest

**Sources:** [Walmart announcement](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/10/walmart-holiday-meal-basket) · October 28, 2026 · [Target announcement](https://corporate.target.com/press/release/2026/10/target-brings-more-value-and-delight-to-thanksgiving-with-a-$30-meal-for-six-and-fresh-takes-on-holi) · October 22

Walmart advertises $35 for ten people, or $3.50 each. Target advertises $30 for six, maintaining its stated $5-per-person value. The assortments differ. Walmart’s estimate also assumes particular produce and turkey weights, with geographic exclusions and possible fulfillment charges.

**Tags:** Price presentation, Price Bundling, Affordability

### 3. Suggested prices influence sellers most when pricing is difficult

**Source:** [“How Effective Is Suggested Pricing?”](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00222437261432198) · March 1, 2026; October issue

A Mercari field experiment found that changing suggested prices by 30% moved listing prices about 5% in the same direction. Suggestions were more influential for new sellers and used goods. Lower suggestions improved sale probability and seller revenue.

**Tags:** Platform pricing, Pricing input

### 4. Cash-versus-card effects survive a large replication, with an important distinction

**Sources:** [“A Mega-Replication … on Pain of Paying”](https://doi.org/10.1093/jcr/ucag027) · August 14, 2026 · [GSB Preserve](https://gsbpreserve.stanford.edu/view/69501/a-mega-replication-of-the-effect-of-cash-versus-card-payment-on-pain-of-paying-magnitude-behavioral-outcomes-and-moderators?limit=50&offset=1&q=facet%2Cmetadata.OA_DC_ACADEMICAREA.en.keyword%2Cequals%2CMarketing&sort=-metadata.OA_DC_DATE.en.keyword)

Thirteen preregistered replications, involving 32,371 participants, supported the finding that cash payment feels more painful than card payment. Payment method had a more reliable and larger direct effect on amount spent than on willingness to pay. The evidence also supported mediation through payment pain and moderation by consumers’ relative use of cash and cards.

**Tags:** Behavioral Economics, Processing Prices

### 5. Charging for the chance to bid works less well when buyers understand it

**Sources:** [“Profitability of two-part tariffs in name-your-own-price markets”](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jretai.2026.01.002) · January 23, 2026; September issue · [anderson.ucla.edu](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/document/2026-05/1-s2.0-S0022435926000023-main.pdf)

Two incentivized laboratory experiments and a field experiment examined nonrefundable fees for submitting a price offer. Small fees could increase seller profits. But experience and a calculation aid reduced participation enough to erode the advantage. The field test involved a short promotional campaign selling university-branded frisbees.

**Tags:** Partitioned Pricing, Comprehension, Assessing profitability

## Why it matters

**1. Halloween shoppers face fewer discounts and more sold-out costumes** My hypothesis: a fixed consumption deadline weakens shoppers’ ability to wait for markdowns, particularly when substitutes disappear. Practitioners should examine availability and remaining shopping time alongside price elasticity. Seasonal urgency is not equivalent to durable pricing power.

**2. Thanksgiving value looks different per basket and per guest** The behavioral mechanism is denominator choice. A lower basket total and a lower per-person cost tell different value stories. My inference: smaller households may prioritize cash outlay, while larger gatherings favor cost per guest. Practitioners should test household fit and expected leftovers, not treat advertised serving counts as interchangeable units.

**3. Suggested prices influence sellers most when pricing is difficult** The pattern is consistent with guidance becoming more valuable under uncertainty, rather than sellers mechanically accepting an anchor. Practitioners should measure completed transactions and revenue, not recommendation acceptance alone. A higher suggested price can raise the listing price while worsening the seller’s eventual outcome.

**4. Cash-versus-card effects survive a large replication, with an important distinction** For practitioners, spending and valuation are not interchangeable outcomes. Checkout design may affect expenditure without producing an equally clear shift in stated reservation prices. These results do not supply a universal sales uplift for accepting cards.

**5. Charging for the chance to bid works less well when buyers understand it** The useful mechanism concerns entry into the deal, not merely bid size. Helping buyers calculate their prospects reduced marginal participation. Practitioners should distinguish a novelty-dependent promotional result from a sustainable pricing model. Short-run profitability can disappear as customers learn.

## Ideas to apply

- In meal-basket tests, show the basket total and cost per serving together. Ask households what they would actually use.
- Evaluate suggested prices using completed transactions and seller revenue as well as the prices sellers post.
- If a promotion charges customers to participate, measure repeat participation after people have learned how it works.
